Grove day 1

Living above the forest

Select a bough using a map label or the site menu. Connect it to the Hearth, preview a building, and confirm its construction. Assign an idle elf to a garden or lodge. Supplies enter storage only when an elf brings them home.

Drag the grove to orbit. Scroll or use + / − to zoom. Turn the canopy off to see routes. Low food slows travel; this prototype does not kill settlers. Host-tree vitality currently reflects supported building weight, not a living ecology simulation.

Save and load use this browser’s local storage; autosave runs every 20 seconds. Export a separate file for safekeeping. Paused and hidden tabs do not simulate offline progress.

Test this build

Diagnostics remain on your device unless you export and share them. Nothing is sent automatically. Test saves, construction and controls on your actual device; logic checks do not test graphics.
